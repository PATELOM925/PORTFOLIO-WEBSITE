## Highlights
- Led a 6-person team to build an auto-grading pipeline (BERT-uncased, spaCy, PyTesseract) with a React front-end and Flask backend, enabling semi-automated grading for handwritten and typed responses.

## Design
The main idea was not to remove human evaluation completely, but to shorten the repetitive parts of grading.

## Stack choices
- BERT-based semantic comparison for meaning-aware answer matching.
- OCR for scanned or handwritten text extraction.
- React + Flask for an end-to-end grading flow.

## Practical constraint
The system worked best as a reviewer assistant, not as a fully autonomous grader.
