## Highlights
- Fine-tuned transformer models (Pegasus, T5, IndicBART) in PyTorch to summarize and translate long-form, multilingual legal text for faster review.
- Packaged the pipeline into a Flask service for repeatable inference and downstream integration.

## Summary
This project focuses on making legal text easier to process through summarization and translation workflows.
