Human-in-the-loop vision workflow

A generic version of the pattern I shipped: the model suggests, a confidence check decides whether a person reviews it, and only reviewed or high-confidence results become audited records. Select a node to see the design decisions.

idle

Core idea

Models are useful but not always right. Routing uncertain suggestions to a person keeps bad predictions out of the records people report on.
